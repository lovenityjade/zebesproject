# Spore Spawn atmosphere

Only room $9DC7 receives the additional effect, through the existing Unreal
presentation material and Engine weather option. Two layers of small, muted
olive/gold spores descend slowly with lateral drift and camera parallax.
Their soft edges are evaluated at display resolution; original pixels remain
nearest-sampled. A light green-brown fog moves behind the combat sprites.

The effect is reduced on sprite silhouettes and excluded from the native HUD,
pause menu and other GUI. Original damaging spores, hitboxes, enemy behavior,
physics, and game RNG are unchanged. Leaving the room clears the room-specific
material parameter; disabling atmosphere or Engine weather removes the effect.

Implementation: `Shaders/Environment.usf`, `Shaders/Present.usf`, the
`SporeAtmosphere` material parameter, and its room binding in `SMHUD.cpp`.
The material is regenerated by `Scripts/prepare-assets.sh`.

An isolated `-SMSporeCheck` fixture renders the actual room on gaming-pc and
captures before/after plus a later animation frame. It freezes the native frame
while advancing only presentation time, so screenshot differences measure the
atmosphere rather than enemy movement. This test requires the isolated-directory
marker and uses private SMTests saves.

Validation completed on gaming-pc: `PLAYTEST_UNREAL_SPORES_PASS`, actual room
`9dc7`, native CPU opcode count zero. Before/after/motion captures were inspected;
the original sprite art remains intact beneath the soft spores and slight fog.
Private review captures are under `.tmp/spore-review/`.

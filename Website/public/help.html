Mission support

Your first landing.

A Windows or Linux PC, the game download, and your own compatible ROM.

  1. Install the game. On Windows, extract the complete ZIP into a writable folder and open SMUnreal.exe. Keep its accompanying folders. On Linux, make the AppImage executable in its file properties, then run it.
  2. Import your ROM. At first launch, choose your file. Once it passes validation, select Confirm, copy ROM and continue. The source file stays where it is; the game keeps its own validated copy.
  3. Choose a save slot. Select Samus A, B or C, then choose Vanilla Mode for the original adventure or Randomizer Mode for a generated seed.
  4. Start your mission. Vanilla is ready to start. For Randomizer, set your options and generate your game first.
Compatible ROM

Super Metroid (Japan, USA) (En,Ja).sfc
CRC32: D63ED5F8 · Size: 3,145,728 bytes.

The name may differ; the contents must match. Use an unmodified, unheadered ROM. No ROM is supplied or downloaded by the game.

How do I change my controls?＋

Open the system menu and select the Input settings. Rebind the keyboard or controller actions there. Button labels in this guide refer to game actions; your chosen bindings may differ.

Can I make Wall Jump and Space Jump easier?＋

Yes. Enable the optional assists under Quality of Life. You can keep the original movement or use the more forgiving versions. Speedrun categories enforce their own rules.

Why are there black borders in fullscreen?＋

Fullscreen controls the window; Image scaling controls how the game fills it. Choose Fit to screen for the largest image that preserves its proportions. Integer scaling keeps uniform pixel blocks and may leave wider margins. Neither option stretches or crops the image.

How do I adjust the effects or music?＋

Graphics settings include atmosphere, lighting, the soft Gaussian blend, parallax, depth, relief and weather. Under Audio, choose Original or Remastered. Remastered playback requires the music pack; missing tracks fall back to Original. The current Windows ZIP includes the selected arrangements.

Mission support

Build your next adventure.

Each save slot can have its own mode and its own seed.

  1. Select an empty slot and choose Randomizer Mode.
  2. Open Randomizer Options in the native game menu. Choose your logic, difficulty, progression, world and completion settings.
  3. Enter a seed number, or leave it empty for a random number. Resolve any settings conflicts reported by the menu.
  4. Return to the native menu and select Generate Game. Wait for generation to finish.
  5. When the seed is ready, Start Game becomes available. The generated seed is saved with the slot.
Why is Start Game grayed out?＋

A randomized slot must have a successfully generated seed before it can start. Select Generate Game after choosing compatible settings. A Vanilla slot does not need generation.

Does the seed number alone reproduce a game?＋

Share both the seed number and the settings string. Use the same game version too: changes to the generator or logic can affect the result. An empty seed field asks the game to choose a number when you generate.

What does No Advanced Techs mean?＋

It restricts the techniques the generator may require. It is separate from combat difficulty, item accessibility and movement assists. Review those settings as well when choosing a gentler run; jump assists do not rewrite the seed’s logic.

Why can generation fail?＋

Some combinations leave no valid progression route or require more resources than the selected item pool provides. Check the settings validation messages first. If generation still fails, save your settings string and the error for a bug report. Do not assume a different number will fix incompatible rules.

How does Chozo Tablet Hunt end?＋

Collect the required tablet quota to trigger an escape to Samus’s ship. The quota can be lower than the number of tablets placed. The final required tablet grants Space Jump, but incoming damage doubles during the escape. Reach and board the ship before the selected timer runs out.

Mission support

Read the world around you.

Tracking is built into the native map and minimap. You do not need a separate PopTracker pack.

What do the markers mean?＋

Squares mark item checks; diamonds mark boss fights. Green means accessible under your seed’s rules. Red means blocked. Yellow indicates an advanced route or uncertain return. Blue means inspect-only where supported. Gray means collected or defeated. A white outline means the result is pending. Multiple checks can share a square.

Does green mean there is a progression item there?＋

No. Green describes whether the check is in logic with your current items and seed settings. It does not promise that the reward unlocks something new. A boss fight can also be the next step in progression, so check boss markers as well as item squares.

Can I use trackers in Vanilla?＋

Yes. Enable Trackers in Vanilla in the tracker settings. It is off by default and uses original item placements; it does not turn your save into a randomized game. Trackers are disabled in speedrun categories.

How does the new Seed Map work?＋

Alpha preview. With shuffled region or boss connections, original map sections are rearranged around the seed’s actual connections. Dotted links show transitions between entrances, not corridors you can walk through. Pan with the D-pad, press Y to cycle zoom levels, A to recenter on Samus, and Select to choose an area. Item-only seeds and Vanilla retain regional maps.

Does door randomization shuffle every single door?＋

No. Current options cover supported region connections, major-boss entrances and a defined set of door colors. Randomizing every door is a requested future extension.

Mission support

Keep your discoveries safe.

Back up the whole save/profile directory, including the seed data and companion files.

Where is my save data?＋

For the Linux AppImage, user data lives in ~/.local/share/zebesproject, or $XDG_DATA_HOME/zebesproject if you set that variable. On Windows, back up the game’s Saved profile directory before replacing the installation. Preserve the complete profile rather than copying only a single SRAM file.

Can Vanilla and Randomizer share a save bank?＋

Yes. A, B and C keep independent modes and seeds. Generating one slot does not randomize the others. Use a new slot or an additional save bank when starting a different adventure.

Why did a save station not refill my energy or ammo?＋

Enable Settings → Quality of Life → Refill energy and ammo when saving, then confirm the station save. Canceling does not refill. Speedrun rules disable this option.

Alpha preview: new installations default this option to on. Existing explicit preferences are preserved, so an older installation may still have it off.

Can I travel between save stations?＋

Alpha preview. Enable Travel between saved stations under Quality of Life. When loading a randomized save, choose a region, then use L / R to select an unlocked station and A / Start to load. This is off by default, uses only stations unlocked in that slot, and is unavailable during escapes or speedruns. Revealing the map does not unlock stations.

Mission support

Back on course.

If something goes wrong, preserve your save and note what happened before retrying.

The ROM is rejected. What should I check?＋

Compare the size and CRC32 with the values above. A headered, patched, pre-randomized, truncated or different-region ROM will not pass. Renaming it does not change its contents. The game checks the local ROM at every startup.

Windows reports a missing DLL or crashes opening Randomizer Options.＋

Use the latest complete Windows ZIP from the release page and extract every folder together. Avoid mixing files from older builds. If the Visual C++ runtime is missing, the package includes Engine/Extras/Redist/en-us/vc_redist.x64.exe. Back up your saves before changing installations. If the problem continues, attach the game log to a bug report.

The Linux AppImage will not start.＋

Check that the file is executable and that your system has a compatible Vulkan graphics driver. The build requires glibc 2.35 or newer. If FUSE is unavailable, launch the AppImage from a terminal with --appimage-extract-and-run. Include the terminal error when asking for help.

Remastered is selected, but the music sounds original.＋

Missing or unavailable tracks automatically use Original. Keep the included Soundtracks/Remastered folder with the Windows installation. Check the Audio panel for a track warning. The Linux AppImage does not include the selected music pack by default.

The tracker seems wrong, or I have no progression left.＋

Check item squares and boss diamonds, along with your seed’s technique, combat and objective settings. An accessible energy or reserve tank is not necessarily a progression reward. If the displayed logic still contradicts what you can do, report the seed number, settings string, inventory, location and a map screenshot. Preserve the save for investigation.

What should I include in a bug report?＋

Include your game version, Windows or Linux version, game mode, seed number and settings string when relevant, exact steps, and a screenshot or short clip. Look for SMUnreal.log in the game’s Saved/Logs directory. Remove personal paths or other sensitive information before sharing logs. Never upload a ROM, soundtrack pack or credentials.

A few more answers.

The essentials about the project and its alpha release.

Is this an official Metroid game?＋

No. The Zebes Project is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or Epic Games. Super Metroid and its characters belong to Nintendo.

Are Story Mode, Boss Rush and Multiworld available?＋

They are planned, not included in the current alpha. Story Mode is planned around scripted cinematics, human voice acting and four endings. Boss Rush is a minimum-equipment time challenge. Multiworld compatibility is planned for SekaiLink: Rebooted and Archipelago.

What are the speedrun rules?＋

Vanilla and New Game+ have separate leaderboards. The native in-game clock determines ranking. Glitches are allowed in both categories. No QoL uses original movement; QoL permits only Wall Jump and Space Jump assists. Save refills, trackers and time savers are not allowed. Debug tools invalidate eligibility. Results require review before publication.

How do I submit a run?＋

Use Submit a run in the website’s leaderboard section. Provide the completed run record, a full-run YouTube or Twitch video and your chosen runner name. A submission stays pending until reviewed; receipt is not approval. Automatic uploads and Discord sign-in are not available yet.

How is AI used?＋

The code is developed by humans with AI assistance. Some current artwork was temporarily generated with AI and is intended to be replaced with human-created art. Credited music and voice recordings are performed by real people; planned Story Mode voice acting is not included in this alpha.

What about flashing lights?＋

The game contains flashes, bright weapon effects and animated lighting. Review the startup warning and adjust the visual options for your comfort. Reducing effects is not a guarantee that all photosensitive triggers are removed.

Where can I follow development?＋

Join Discord for discussion and feedback, or follow GitHub for source, releases and issue reports.

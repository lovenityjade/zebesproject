The rules, at a glance ＋

Your in-game clock decides your rank. Real time is shown for reference. Glitches are allowed in both categories.

No QoL: original movement, no assists. QoL: only Wall Jump and Space Jump assists are allowed. Save refills, time savers and trackers are disabled for speedruns. Debug tools invalidate a run.

Runs are reviewed before appearing here. One personal best per runner, mode and category; equal in-game times share a rank. New Game+ has a separate board because it carries equipment from a previous completion.

During the alpha, submit your completed run record and a full-run video for review. Automatic in-game uploads and Discord sign-in are still to come.

Before you land

A few things to know.

Open the player guide & full FAQ ↗

What do I need to play?＋

A Windows or Linux PC and your own compatible Super Metroid ROM. The expected file is Super Metroid (Japan, USA) (En,Ja).sfc, 3,145,728 bytes, CRC32 D63ED5F8. The filename can differ; the game validates the contents. Read the setup guide ↗.

Can I keep the original gameplay and music?＋

Yes. Start a Vanilla game and choose the comfort and visual options you want. Jump assists and save refills are optional. Original music remains available; the Windows download also includes the selected Remastered arrangements with full artist credits.

Is this an official Metroid release?＋

No. The Zebes Project is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or Epic Games. Super Metroid and its characters belong to Nintendo. You must provide your own compatible game ROM.

What should I know about flashing effects?＋

The game contains bright flashes, weapon effects and animated lighting that may affect people with photosensitivity. Visual intensity and comfort options are available in-game. The website’s Motion control pauses its decorative animation and respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.

How are AI tools used in the project?＋

The code is developed by humans with AI assistance. Some current artwork is temporary AI-generated material, intended to be replaced with human-created art. The credited music and voice recordings are performed by real people. Planned Story Mode voice acting is not part of this alpha.

What is published when I submit a speedrun?＋

Your chosen runner name, category, times and video link become public only after review. Submitted records are kept for moderation; a keyed network fingerprint limits spam and expires from active rate-limit records after 24 hours on the next submission. We do not ask for a ROM, save file, email address or account password. Submit only a video you are comfortable sharing publicly. Contact the project for a correction or removal request.

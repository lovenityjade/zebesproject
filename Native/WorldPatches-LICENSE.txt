Native adaptations of VARIA start, layout, boss-connection, colored-door and gameplay tweak patches.
Sources: Randomizer/upstream/patches/common, patches/vanilla, graph and rom; pinned revision
72ec1f30b700442d0c30aad6c43801bd64f1e2a5.

Bomb Torizo awakening: original patch by PJBoy, adapted for VARIA.
Beam doors: original IPS by Mettyk25jigsaw, disassembled and factorized by
VARIA contributors; native C hit-check counterparts and unchanged PLM/art data.
Red missile-only doors: VARIA Randomizer contributors.
Other start/layout patches: VARIA Randomizer contributors; individual
source files and exact source spans are recorded in the world catalog audit.
No executable SNES instructions from these patches run in the C port.
